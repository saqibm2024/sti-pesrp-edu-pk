STI Online Application 2026 – Apply Online at sti.pesrp.edu.pk

Easy guide to registration, eligibility, documents, application steps, merit lists and STI Jobs 2026.

The STI Online Application 2026 process is available through the official sti.pesrp.edu.pk portal. Eligible candidates can use the portal to register and apply for the School Teacher Internship Program in Punjab.

Applicants need to create an account or log in first. They then enter their personal details, education information, district details and other required information. After uploading the documents, they should check the full form before submitting it.

This guide explains the STI application process in simple English. It also covers eligibility, required documents, important dates, merit lists and common mistakes.

What Is the STI Online Application Portal?

The STI portal is managed by the Punjab School Education Department through the Program Monitoring & Implementation Unit (PMIU-PESRP). It is used for applications for School Teacher Interns in government schools across Punjab.

Through the portal, candidates can:

  • Create an online account
  • Enter educational information
  • Select schools and subjects
  • Upload required documents
  • Check application status and merit lists

The online system is designed to make the application and selection process easier and more transparent.

Documents Required for STI Registration

Prepare clear copies of these documents before starting the online form:

  • CNIC
  • Domicile certificate
  • Recent passport-size photograph
  • Educational certificates from Matric to the latest qualification
  • Teaching experience certificate, if applicable

Make sure the files are clear and meet the portal's file size and format rules.

Step-by-Step STI Online Application 2026

Step 1: Visit the Official Website

Open https://sti.pesrp.edu.pk. Check the latest notice and find the online application option when the application window is open.

Step 2: Create an Account

Click the registration option and enter your CNIC, email, password and mobile number. Complete any verification required by the portal.

Step 3: Log In

Use your CNIC and password to enter your dashboard. The dashboard is where you manage your application.

Step 4: Add Personal Details

Enter your name, date of birth, address, gender and contact information. Check spelling and numbers carefully.

Step 5: Add Education Details

Enter your Matric, Intermediate, Bachelor’s, Master’s and other relevant education details. Add subjects, passing years, total marks and obtained marks where required.

Step 6: Select School and Subject

Select your district and tehsil, then choose the school level and subject that match your qualification and the available options.

Step 7: Upload Documents

Upload your CNIC, domicile, photograph and education certificates. Use clear files and follow the portal's format and size limits.

Step 8: Review and Submit

Check every field before final submission. Once the form is correct, submit the application and save the confirmation information.

Step 9: Save the Application Slip

Download or print the application slip and keep it for your records.

Step 10: Check Application Status

Log in to your dashboard later to check your application status and merit list when the department publishes it.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Wrong CNIC or email: Use correct and active information.
  • Unclear documents: Upload readable files.
  • Wrong subject: Select a subject related to your qualification.
  • Missing education details: Complete all required academic fields.
  • Late submission: Submit before the official closing date.

Benefits of Applying Online

  • Easy access: Apply using a mobile phone or computer.
  • Time saving: The application can be completed online.
  • Transparency: Merit information can be published through the online system.
  • Confirmation: Applicants can keep their submission record.
  • Equal opportunity: The online system supports merit-based selection.

What Happens After Submission?

After the application period closes, the Education Department will check the applications. Merit lists will be published according to the official schedule.

Candidates may be able to submit corrections or complaints during the announced objection period. After this process, the final merit list will be issued and selected interns will join their assigned schools according to the official instructions.

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I apply online for STI Jobs 2026?

Visit sti.pesrp.edu.pk and complete the online form when the department opens the application window.

Can I apply for more than one school level?

The source information says candidates can apply for one level according to their qualification: Primary, Elementary, Secondary or Higher Secondary.

What if the STI portal is not opening?

Try again later, especially during less busy hours. You can also clear your browser cache and check your internet connection.

Is there an application fee?

Check the official advertisement for the current fee information before applying.

How will I know if I am selected?

Check your dashboard and the official merit list after it is published.

What happens after selection?

Selected interns will report to their assigned schools according to the official joining schedule.

Do I need to submit hard copies?

Follow the instructions in the official advertisement. The source information notes that earlier cycles used digital uploads.

Can I edit the form after final submission?

The source information says the form cannot be edited after final submission, so check all details carefully before submitting.
